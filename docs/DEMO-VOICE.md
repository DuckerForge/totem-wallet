## How to read it

- One idea per sentence. The full stops are where the picture changes.
- Say the name twice in the whole film: the first line and the last. Nowhere else.
- Numbers are said plainly: "ten thousand", not "10k". "Five by five", not "5x5".
- Nothing here is a claim the app cannot back: no on-chain stop loss, forgotten money is
  found and not collected, the collar is enforced on the phone. Keep it that way.
